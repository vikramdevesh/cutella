Sustainability commitments

Our hazelnuts, our cocoa, our promise

100% traceable hazelnuts. 100% certified cocoa. 0% palm oil.

We work directly with 2,400 hazelnut farmers and 11 cocoa co-operatives. By 2030 our Pune plant will run on 100% renewable electricity, and we're already at 68% thanks to rooftop solar.

Quality & ingredients

Seven ingredients. Nothing to hide.

Sugar, roasted hazelnuts (13%), sunflower oil, skimmed milk powder (8.7%), fat-reduced cocoa (7.4%), soy lecithin, vanillin.

Every batch is tasted by a real human before it's jarred. Yes, that's a job. Yes, it's the best one.

Recycling the jar

Rinse. Separate. Recycle.

  1. Scrape out the last bit (with a biscuit, obviously).
  2. Rinse the jar with warm water.
  3. Separate the lid (PP plastic) and the paper label.
  4. Drop the glass in the glass bin — or give it a new life.

Our heritage

From a Shimla kitchen to your breakfast table

1978

A Shimla kitchen

Grandmother Kamala Mehra blends roasted hill hazelnuts with cocoa for her grandchildren's tiffins.

1986

The first jar

The 'Cutella' name is coined by a seven-year-old who couldn't say 'cute Nutella-like spread'. It sticks.

1999

Moving to Pune

Our first proper factory opens in Pune, producing 2,000 jars a day.

2012

Palm-oil free, officially

We certify what we always did: zero palm oil, sunflower oil only.

2021

Dunk Biscuits

Cutella Dunk Biscuits launch — shaped exactly to fit the jar's mouth.

2026

A fresh new look

A new label, a lighter glass jar and the first Cutella Café.
