Our hazelnuts

Every hazelnut, traced back to its tree

100% of our hazelnuts are traceable to partner farms in Himachal Pradesh and the Black Sea coast, roasted in small batches in Pune.

We pay a fair-harvest premium to 2,400 partner farmers and support them with free agronomy training. And yes — Cutella has always been made without palm oil.

Discover more
Our cocoa

Cocoa that does good, all the way down

All Cutella cocoa is certified by the (dummy) Fair Cocoa Alliance, and by 2027 we'll be sourcing 50% of it from Kerala and Andhra Pradesh growers.

Our cocoa programme funds 40 village schools and child-labour monitoring across every co-operative we buy from.
