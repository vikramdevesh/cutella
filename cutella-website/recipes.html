Up-cycle your Cutella jar

Our glass jars are sturdy, food-safe and far too pretty for the bin. Here are six favourite ideas from the Cutella community.

Share yours with #CutellaJarLife and we might feature it!

🌿

Kitchen herb garden

Fill with pebbles, soil and a basil cutting. Sunny windowsill, water twice a week.

✏️

Desk organiser

Wrap the jar in twine or washi tape and keep pens, brushes and scissors tidy.

🥣

Overnight-oats glass

Oats, milk, a spoon of Cutella — refrigerate overnight and grab on the way out.

🕯️

Fairy-light lantern

Pop in a string of battery fairy lights for an instant Diwali glow.

🧂

Spice & dry-fruit store

Airtight lid, clear glass — perfect for almonds, cashews or masala.

🪙

Savings jar

Cut a slot in the lid and start saving for your next Dunk Kit.
